Draig Dental Compliance

Built for HIW inspections, not English ones.

Dental practices in Wales are inspected by Healthcare Inspectorate Wales (HIW), yet the compliance products on the market are built around inspections in England. Draig Dental Compliance is designed around HIW from the start, so practices know exactly what inspectors will look for, and can show it.

It also includes full HR and group management, so a practice owner can run compliance, staff and every site from one place.

  • Built around HIW inspection requirements
  • Full HR management
  • Group management across multiple practices
  • Designed for dental practices in Wales

GameMurder mystery18+

A new murder every time you play.

A detective game set in present-day Wales, where every witness and suspect talks back.

A body has been found. You’re the Detective Sergeant, and you have 72 hours to find the killer.

No two cases are ever the same. Every time you play, the game builds a brand-new murder: a different victim, killer, motive and method, with a fresh trail of evidence. It could be a fall in a Snowdonia slate quarry, a hit and run in the Rhondda or a poisoning near Tenby. You can’t look up the answer, because nobody knows it but the killer.

Question people in your own words. Witnesses and suspects are AI characters with their own memories, secrets and alibis, and they answer whatever you ask, in English or Welsh. Some tell the truth. Some lie. Put the right evidence in front of them and watch their stories fall apart.

Send exhibits to the forensic lab and wait for DNA, fibres and phone records to come back. Travel between real Welsh locations, bring suspects in for interview, and build a case strong enough for the Crown Prosecution Service. Charge the wrong person and the killer walks free.

Features

  • A new murder mystery every time you play
  • AI witnesses and suspects who answer anything you ask, and who can lie
  • Seven ways to kill, each with its own forensic trail
  • Real locations across Wales: Mumbles, Treorchy, Llandeilo, Llanberis and Tenby
  • Fully playable in English or Welsh
  • Forensic lab results that take time, so every hour counts
  • Works on desktop and tablet

18For players aged 18 and over. Contains depictions of violent crime.

GameTown building

Valley Life

Build a Welsh town from the ground up.

Valley Life is a town-building game set in the valleys of Wales. Start in 1800 with a Market Hall, a muddy track and three horse-drawn carts. From there, grow a living town through the Industrial Age and on into the modern day. Every map is new, with its own hills, rivers and Welsh place names.

Features

  • A town that’s really alive: every resident appears on the map. Adults walk to the pit or the mill, children go to school, and families head to chapel on Sundays.
  • Industry and trade: sink coal pits and quarry slate, then build ironworks and factories. Your lorries carry goods to market, and a railway opens up exports.
  • History in every issue: a monthly newspaper reports real events from Welsh history, with engraved pictures and links to read more.
  • Look after your people: shops, clinics, schools and chapels keep families happy and the town growing.
  • Weather the storms: floods, earthquakes, market slumps and wars test every town planner.
  • Fully bilingual: switch between English and Welsh at any time.
  • Play anywhere: in your browser on desktop or mobile, in 2D or 3D, with original music that’s composed as you play.

Valley Life. Your valley, your story.

Ironworks

Build the steam age. Then defend it.

Ironworks is a steampunk factory-building game that runs in your browser. You start with a single brass hub and a patch of iron ore. From there you build drills, furnaces, belts and assemblers until your factory runs itself. It’s a factory game with real mechanics and a gentle learning curve.

Lay belts across hills and valleys, feed ore into brick furnaces and route plates to clockwork assemblers. Draw water from lakes to fire boilers and turn steam engines. Research your way through six eras, from coal and copper wire to oil refining, railways, drones, solar power and a rocket launch to orbit.

Your factory isn’t alone. Choose how dangerous your world is:

  • crawlers that swarm out of their nests when your smog spreads
  • lightning storms
  • earthquakes that crack the ground
  • a red dragon that circles your works and breathes fire on whatever it finds

Build steam turrets to bring it down and claim its hoard.

Features

  • Full 3D world: a camera that turns all the way round, with real lighting and shadows. The original 2D view is still available as Classic mode.
  • Six eras of research: over 40 buildings, from hand-fed furnaces to centrifuges and launch pads.
  • Railways, drones and fluid networks: move goods further and faster as your factory grows.
  • Hazards you choose: pick from Peaceful to Harsh, or set each hazard yourself.
  • Quality of life: undo, copy and paste, saved layouts, production statistics, alerts and a minimap.
  • Bilingual: play in English or Cymraeg (as Gwaith Haearn), and switch at any time.
  • Accessibility: resource symbols for players who don’t rely on colour, a calm mode that turns off screen shake, and keyboard controls throughout.
  • Original soundtrack: generative music that never plays the same way twice.
