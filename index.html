Website questions

Anything else, just ask. A short call is the quickest way to get a clear answer.

How long does a website take?

It depends on the package and how ready your content is. You get a dated plan before you commit, so you know when each stage lands.

Can I make changes after launch?

Yes. Tell us what you would like changed and we will quote it up front, or we can talk about adding an editor if you expect to update the site often.

Do you handle domains and hosting?

We set up hosting and connect your domain, in your name. You keep full ownership and can move it whenever you like.

I already have a website. Can you rebuild it?

Yes. We review what works on your current site, keep the pages that bring in visitors, and redirect old links so you do not lose search ranking.

What if my site needs to grow into an app later?

That is where a studio that also builds SaaS and apps helps. We structure the site so new features can be added without starting again.
